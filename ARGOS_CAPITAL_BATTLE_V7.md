# Argos V7 — Capital Battle & Target Continuation

Adds a visual capital battle timeline and capital-efficiency view to the existing two-sided money engine.

## Dashboard
- LONG/SHORT aggressive traded notional percentages
- rolling capital-battle timeline
- pressure durability, erosion and resistance
- capital efficiency: price response relative to aggressive traded capital
- live target and target-extension count
- target-aftercare state: CONTINUE / RESISTANCE / WATCH

The percentages represent observed aggressive taker flow, not the amount of open long/short positions. Order-book liquidity is shown separately because resting orders can be cancelled.
